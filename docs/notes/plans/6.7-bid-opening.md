## 0. What the code says today

- `ExerciseStatus` is DRAFT / DOCUMENTS_IN_PREPARATION / CANCELLED. 6.4 adds PENDING_/APPROVED_FOR_TENDERING, 6.5 ADVERTISED / INVITED.
  Every map over it is exhaustive (X-8), so each new value fans out to labels, tones, transitions, `documents/attach.ts`, the award
  gate, the sourcing list and the dashboard.
- **Committees exist** (6.10): `Committee`, `CommitteeMember` (person or office seat, effective dates, alternates), quorum and voting
  rule nullable. No opening committee is in the demo (`demo_c1`–`c5`). `Person.department` is a free label; there is no person→unit
  link, which is why FR-CTE-011 was deferred.
- **The physical box is the control** (6.6 OD-1 A): no bid content exists in the database before opening. NFR-SEC-003 and the
  electronic box are re-designated to Phase 3 with sign-in and D6. There is no sign-in, so every act here is "recorded on behalf of,
  not signed".
- **The prototype** (`docs/prototype/RSAJ-Tender-Portal.html`, `sr.op.*` strings; no per-screen spec exists for opening) holds: a
  panel card with "n of 2 confirmed", "Open the responses", late responses with Admit / Set aside, an opening record in opened order,
  the CPD/J.TAWARAN-02 print (Bil., Pentender, Gred, Harga, Tempoh), "the spread" against the internal estimate, minutes. **Not
  reproduced:** a panel "Admit / Set aside" of late responses (FR-BID-003, R11: the Tender Board alone decides), the shared password,
  three members chaired inside C&P, browser-stored decisions, hard-coded signatories and late count. Comparators record each in
  `DECIDED_FIELDS`.

## 4. Actions (`src/app/sourcing/[id]/opening/actions.ts`)

Lock order: requisition → exercise → `ExerciseWindow` → `Opening` → envelope / opened-bid row (6.5/6.6 order, extended). Policy,
roster, closing, box events, withdrawals and sales are re-read inside the transaction and the pure check re-run on locked data. Every
write is audited before/after in the same transaction. Persons are "recorded on behalf of, not signed". Codes back, never figures.

1. `startOpening` (panel, attendance, lead check or override → LATE_OVERRIDE event, checklist and policy snapshots).
2. `recordAuthorisation`: one Person per call; the second distinct present member writes `BoxEvent` UNLOCKED_FOR_OPENING and
   `ExerciseStatus` → OPENING_IN_PROGRESS.
3. `registerBoxEnvelope` (6.6 helper with channel BOX, `ReceiptSource` BOX_AT_OPENING, late = false by the LOCKED event; a sequence
   from 6.6's allocator), `matchWithdrawal`, `recordWithdrawalFinding`.
4. `openEnvelope`: listing no. under the opening lock; party picked from `ExerciseParty` or created as BIDDER_AT_OPENING; offer as
   read; findings computed in lib; envelope → OPENED. **The only writer of `BidEnvelope.partyId`** (6.6 OD-2 source scan moves here).
5. `recordInitial`, `recordChecklistAnswer`, `recordVerdict`, `correctAsRead` (before finalisation only; reason; before/after
   audited; OD-10).
6. `checkEstimateCopy`: the officer types the figure on the paper copy; compared through `readSealedFigures`; only MATCH / MISMATCH
   is stored and returned (the typed figure is not stored or echoed). **SULIT exposure, weighed:** a verdict lets whoever calls it
   confirm a guess of the sealed figure, the same shape as doc-store carry D2 (C-6, identical re-upload confirms a guess). So it is
   callable only while OPENING_IN_PROGRESS (the paper copy is already open on the table), every attempt writes an audit row with
   no figure, and the attempt count shows on the Opening tab beside the verdict.
7. `recordSignature`.
8. `finaliseOpening`: `finaliseFaults` on locked data; 6.2 `disclose()` + DISCLOSED event; freeze schedule and Grand Summary;
   `ExerciseStatus` → OPENED. Then the scan through the generic doc-store attach (owner `opening`, purpose SIGNED_SCHEDULE, SULIT at
   upload, refused before finalising). `adjournOpening` / `resumeOpening` (reason; nothing is lost, FR-OPN-011).
9. **Award gate (flagged edit to 6.11's gate as left by 6.5/6.6, X-9):** for an exercise published through 6.5 with a TENDER/
   QUOTATION pack, the award needs the MAIN opening finalised with its scan attached, no LATE_TENDER undecided (6.6), no admitted envelope unopened, and the
   awardee's registration number among `evaluationSet()`. 6.8 tightens it. New tests only.
10. `startSupplementaryOpening` (from a CARRIED LATE_TENDER matter; same panel rules; steps 2, 4–8 on that envelope). If 6.8 has
    issued a report, finalising it supersedes that report and opens evaluation version n+1 (6.8 §4.8), in the same transaction.
11. **Cross-module, flagged:** 6.5 `cancelFaults` and the exercise cancel accept OPENING_IN_PROGRESS / OPENED (the exit for an empty
    box or every bid rejected); X-8 fan-out for the two statuses. Configuration: committee panel fields, method fields,
