# 6.13 Part B, run 1b (time and damages): design note

**Status: phase A.** Written 2026-10-04 at aa3fa07, before any run 1b code beyond commit 0 (68347d5), for the code
reviewer's check before screens (process step 2). The ruling trace is the run's decisions-log entry ("Run 1b (time and
damages), phase A: the ruling trace"). Plan: `6.13-post-award-lifecycle.md` Part B, B4 run 1, B5, B6, B10 run 1b.
ADRs 0029 (the matter), 0030 (extensions and the frozen basis), 0031 (the act × status table).

Every act below is one transaction under `withLockedContract`, reads the server's clock once on entry (to the second),
refuses CANCELLED first and then its family's `ACT_STATUS` row (`actStatusFault`, in the table's words), re-runs its pure
rule on the locked rows, writes, and audits before and after in the same transaction. A refusal throws, so nothing is
half-written. Nobody is signed in: each act records the Person the officer names, "recorded on behalf of, not signed".
Days are the organisation's days; every window's end is exclusive. Money is sen (`bigint`) in the rules and Decimal in
the database; a decimal becomes text only through `decimalText`/`senToDecimalString`; an exact band goes through
`percent-band.ts`. No figure from the specification is in a computation (BD-613-102): every one is a setting or a
parameter, and the spec's figures are test fixtures.

## 1. The lock order

    Requisition → SourcingExercise → Award → Contract            (withLockedContract)
      → Certificate                                              (where the act is a certificate's: withLockedCertificate)
        → ContractMatter                                         (the first domain row; ascending id where several)
          → the matter's own record (EotClaim | PeriodExtension | LossExpenseClaim | LadWaiver)
            → its children and other domain rows (EotReason, DelayNotice, NonCompletion, InterimValuation, …)
              → CommitteeMeeting (the sitting, where a committee matter is tabled or decided)
                → DocumentTemplate FOR SHARE (a pin)
                  → Supplier (last; none in run 1b)

- A certificate's act (sign, issue) holds the Certificate before it reads the matter, so the matter hook and the issue
  hook read ContractMatter after the Certificate, as the order says. An act that raises a certificate (the EOT
  certificate, the period agreement, an interim applying waivers) locks the matter first and then creates the
  certificate row, which no other transaction can hold yet.
- The interim's raise, where it applies waivers, locks each waiver's ContractMatter (ascending id) and LadWaiver row
  before the InterimValuation (the matter is the first domain row). Part A's raise locked the valuation first; it now
  takes the waivers' rows first. Stricter, flagged against Part A in C12, and pinned by lock-order.test.ts.
- `/approvals`' escalation (BD-613-117) gains the ContractMatter line in the chain order: Requisition, Exercise, Award,
  Contract, ContractMatter, then reads the matter's family row (`MATTER_FAMILY[kind]`) of `ACT_STATUS`; it restates no
  state list.
- The committee outcome on a CONTRACT matter (`recordOutcome`) takes Contract → ContractMatter → the sitting, the order
  tabling takes, and asks the matter's family row there (the S-3d precedent for a variation's matter). BD-613-131 (the
  review's dissent on gating an outcome) is referred back for ruling; whatever is ruled for VARIATION is applied to
  CONTRACT matters in the same commit. Run 1b writes no contract status, so no family here is refused in any state the
  contract can reach in this run.

### 6.10 The interim (flagged against Part A; a shared rule's output, full suite)

The raise (VALUATION) assembles one damages line per scope with a live decision (`lad/scopes.ts`): each by rate

whole-works line keeps Part A's figure keys (`ladRate`, `ladFrom`, …, `ladAmount`); further scopes add keys suffixed by

(`payment/at-issue.ts`) refuses LAD_BASIS_MOVED ("The damages it was raised on have moved since: {moved}. Void it and

## 7. 613A-C2 as approved (BD-613-89, BD-613-75)

- **Basis.** Every Part A decision reads CNC_DETERMINATION and stays live and readable until replaced, labelled
  "Entitlement determined on the non-completion decision, before extension-of-time records" / "Kelayakan diputuskan
  pada keputusan kerja tidak siap, sebelum rekod lanjutan masa". Every new one is EOT_RECORD.
- **Supersession.** At the EOT certificate's issue every live decision on a scope the extension moves is superseded. A
  later valuation whose day is past that scope's new date is refused NON_COMPLETION_SUPERSEDED until a replacement is
  recorded (Part A's replacement path, with its reason). Where the day is on or before the new date, nothing is asked:
  the decision is closed by the extension, its note recorded at the issue ("Closed by the extension of time {number}:
  the new date for completion {day} is not yet past." / "Ditutup oleh lanjutan masa {number}: tarikh siap baharu {day}
  belum berlalu."), and nothing is charged on it again; a decision is recorded afresh only if the new date is later
  missed. Issued interims are untouched.
- **Credit.** Per issued damages line, per rate segment: the days of `[from, until)` inside `[previous + 1, new + 1)` of
  each issued extension on its scope (or on the whole works), less what earlier credits returned of that segment, ×
  that segment's rate. The rounding is never returned; a credit is never keyed. 269.38 from 1 Sep, 1–30 Sep charged;
  EOT 14 days from 31 Aug → 14 Sep: 14 × 269.38 = 3,771.32.
- **The cap**, per damages line (FR-LAD-011's one damages line per scope: what that scope charged on every issued
  certificate and this one; corrected at C5 from "per scope's decision", which lines issued before run 1b cannot be read
  by): credits plus waivers applied never exceed the damages charged. At exactly equal it passes; one sen over refuses.
  Build decision for ruling, **BD-613-136**: a credit refused by the cap has no exit in the product (an applied waiver is
  on an issued certificate and is never undone), so the interim cannot be raised until a person resolves it outside the
  product. The ruled refusal is built; proposed alternative for ruling: cap the credit at what remains, with a warning
  on the certificate naming the line.
