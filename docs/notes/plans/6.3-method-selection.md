## 0. Findings in today's code

- **One axis only.** `AuthorityMatrix` holds `quotationFloor`, `tenderFloor`, `requisitionBoundary` and `boundaryValue`; `regimeFor` gives below/quotation/tender and already passes the switch explicitly (`inBand(…, policy, true)`).
  - There is no terms band.
  - `requisitionBoundary` is loaded and displayed, but no decision reads it. 6.3 does not read it either: the requisition family is the `domain=requisition` rules (6.1).
- **`boundaryValue` and `tenderFloor` say the same thing.** `saveMatrix` already refuses a boundary that differs from the tender floor, inline in the action. Per ruling it moves to `src/lib` and stays a hard reject.
- **6.1 made the switch per rule.** `inBand(value, min, max, policy, follows = true)`; a rule passes its own `followsBoundarySwitch`; `boundaryRuleFault` refuses an undecided boundary edge on save; `BoundaryOperator` (GT/GTE) is already an enum and is snapshotted on `ApprovalInstance`. Requisition rules do not follow the switch.
- **`ApprovalInstance` is generic.** `matterKind`, nullable `requisitionId`, rule/boundary/funder snapshots, steps decided by a named Person via `deciderFaults`. 6.3 adds `exerciseId` and uses it for name approval.
- **`SourcingMethod` has regime flags but no behaviours.** No default per regime, no justification, proposal, name approval, restriction or emergency record. `ContractType` is a label only. `Requisition.contractTypeCode` (6.1) is the requester's indication.
- **Domain keys disagree.** The demo's first rules use domain `purchase` (and 6.10's demo matter cites `demo.purchase.high`). 6.3 uses `appointment` for the method family; the `purchase` demo rows are left as they are (build decision BD-3).
- **No exercise exists.** `/sourcing` is `NotBuilt`. `MatterKind.EXERCISE` exists (6.10).
- **The spec contradicts itself on basis** (FR-MTD-013 vs 014) — ruled below.
- **The taxonomies do not map 1:1.** Three canonical types cannot determine seven report forms, so mapping needs discriminators and an AMBIGUOUS state.
- **D13 asymmetry.** A direct appointment escalates to Chairman/Board; a tender never does. Configuration; a test asserts it, nothing "fixes" it.
- **Demo parents.** The approved demo requisitions are small (rq7 RM18,500, rq9 RM800 supervision-only, rq10 RM15,000 received from outside, rq12 RM62,000). Exercises above those values need new approved parents (§7).

Rules for writes:
- **Locks.** Every exercise write runs in one transaction that locks the parent **Requisition** row (`FOR UPDATE`), then the exercise row, re-reads both, reads the matrix issue, methods, types, policy, roster and numbering inside it, and re-runs the pure check before writing. Sibling sums therefore never race.
- **Audit.** Every write (exercise, children, the requisition's status move, name-approval instance and steps) writes its audit row in the same transaction. Reasons are required for cancel, method override, type override.
- **Creating an exercise** is refused unless `openFault` passes: the requisition is APPROVED or SOURCING, `sourcingPermitted` is true, it carries an approved route, the organisation's time zone is set, and a sourcing method list is configured.
- **The first move to DOCUMENTS_IN_PREPARATION** moves the requisition APPROVED → SOURCING through 6.1's `transitionFault` and a status-guarded update, audited.
- **Cancellation** is labelled an assumption on screen (§7.3.2 gives no SOP path for it).
- **Contract type on the requisition** (6.1) pre-fills the choice only as the requester's indication; the exercise's value is the controlled one.

## 8. Build decisions

Taken during the build, most consequential first. None contradicts a ruling; each is for the product owner to confirm.

- **BD-1 · An exercise may be opened on a requisition already in Sourcing.** §7.3.2 says "created against an approved requisition"; the first exercise to move into preparation takes the requisition APPROVED → SOURCING, and a sibling may still be opened afterwards (the sum rule R-16 still binds). A cancelled exercise leaves the requisition in Sourcing: 6.1 has no SOURCING → APPROVED move.
- **BD-2 · What a draft may be saved with.** A draft saves half-written (as 6.1's requisitions do) but refuses anything it gives that is not real: value over the approved route (R-16), no/retired/out-of-regime method, an override without its reason (R-12), a scope split that does not sum to the value, a chosen contract type without reason and Person, an unknown basis or reason. Everything else that blocks (justification, reason code, coordination record, unreviewed proposal, ambiguous classification, basis unset) blocks only the move into preparation. `SAVE_BLOCKERS` in `src/lib/method/evaluate.ts`.
- **BD-3 · Appointment with no rule warns, it does not block.** A method with no approval rule at the value, or no approval-matrix key, is recorded and shown as a gap ("gaps stay honest") rather than stopping classification; the approver is needed at award (6.11), which must refuse it.
- **BD-4 · Name approval does not gate preparation.** Per R-13 (OD-8 B) it gates invitation only; a selected tender can move into preparation with its name pending.
- **BD-5 · A change of method or value on a draft withdraws an open or approved name-approval route** (closed as withdrawn, with the reason, audited): it approved a different exercise. A change of method re-opens the proposal as a draft; answers are kept.
- **BD-6 · The same person may not discharge two steps of the name route** (6.1's rule, reused). The spec's "MR/P by C&P" is therefore configured in the demo as a proposing office (Section Head, Pre-contract) and a recommending one (Head of C&P); a customer whose one office both proposes and recommends needs a delegation or a rule change.
- **BD-7 · A name route with a committee step is refused with its exit** (`NAME_ROUTE_HAS_COMMITTEE_STEP`): exercises cannot yet be tabled at a sitting (CommitteeDecision is not linked to exercises until 6.5). The message says to record it outside the portal or change the rule.
- **BD-8 · Name-approval deciders:** 6.1's holder, delegation, date and one-step-per-person checks, with the requisition's requester barred (nobody decides the name for an exercise on a requisition they raised). Holders are found for the requisition's unit.
- **BD-9 · Numbering:** exercise series `exercise`; dossier references issued from the method's `dossierSeriesKey` when the exercise moves into preparation (a cancelled draft never consumes one). Demo ids `demo_n5`/`demo_n6` because `demo_n4` is already the sittings series.
- **BD-10 · One default method per band and one contract type per deliverable** are refused on save in Reference data (otherwise the regime/deliverable test proposes nothing). A method that asks for a proposal must also ask for approval of the name (the proposal goes to the name's approver).
- **BD-11 · Taxonomy NOT_MAPPED warns; AMBIGUOUS blocks preparation** until the officer's one pick is stored.
- **BD-12 · The requester's contract-type indication is shown, not pre-filled** on the exercise: pre-filling would make it an override needing a reason.
- **BD-13 · Demo:** the `purchase`-domain demo rows (6.10's sittings cite them) are left alone; the method family is added as `appointment` rows. Two approved parents (`demo_rq13` RM330,000, `demo_rq14` RM600,000) carry a full 6.1 trail on a new invented budget code and account so their split checks are honestly clear; the plan's use of rq7/rq10 was not possible at their values. Offices Quotation Committee, Chairman and Technical Committee added as demo roles. Exercises are determined by the same lib functions a save uses, and the load refuses if one would not save.
