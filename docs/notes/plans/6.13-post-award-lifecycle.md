**Status: draft for the product owner's go (2026-10-01).** No code written. Written against `174cc1e` on `build-all-modules`.
Spec: §6.13 (FR-SUP, PAY, VAR, PAD, EOT, LAD, TRM, CMP, NSC, FIN), §7.1–7.3.3, §8.3–8.9, §6.9.3 examples 7–14, §9 INT-06/07,

**Scope mapping (correct here in one line if wrong).** The baseline (FR-CON-014) is 6.12's and lands first; 6.13 reads it through
`baselineFor(contract, on)` and never re-keys a baseline value. **Order: 6.12 → 6.14 increment 1 (certificate core) → 6.13 Part A →
6.14 increment 2 → 6.13 Part B** (same sentence in all three plans): Part A's first certificate is 4b's delegation letter. **Part A (Phase 4)** = §13.6 4b supervision (§6.13.1), 4c interim payment (§6.13.2), 4d variations and price
adjustment (§6.13.3–4), **plus the minimal LAD chain** (below). **Part B (Phase 5)** = §6.13.5–6.13.10. Part A is accepted as one unit:
4c and 4d are not accepted apart (§13.9 "variations and contract price adjustment ship together"; FR-PAY-008 needs 4d).

**§13.9 pairs that straddle the Phase 4/5 line, resolved here:**
- *Interim payment + LAD set-off.* FR-LAD-001–008 (warning letter, Certificate of Non-Completion, LAD line) move into Part A. A
  certificate on a contract past its Date for Completion with no CNC decision is refused, naming "record the non-completion decision"
  (never issued silently without LAD). Until Part B's EOT module, the CNC records the entitlement determination itself; Part B replaces
  it with the EOT record (stricter, flagged).
- *Retention release.* Part A keeps the running balance and the 50% release on as-built drawings; the balance release is refused until
  a Certificate of Making Good Defects exists, and says it arrives with Part B. That is a precondition, not a dead state: the contract
  carries on.
- *Variations + PPJHK.* Both in Part A; interim certificates read the revised sum only from issued PPJHK certificates (FR-PAD-003).
