## 4. Actions (writes are few; every report is read-only)

1. Configuration (`src/app/configuration/service-levels/actions.ts`): save and retire `ApprovalTarget` (retire asks a reason),
   `CycleStageTarget`, `ReportPolicy`; uniqueness re-checked in the transaction; audited before/after.
2. **Overdue escalation (own flagged commit to 6.9's escalation action, new tests only):** offered when `ageState` is OVERDUE or
   ESCALATION_DUE, reason pre-filled "Overdue by N days against a target of T days". Locks route then step (existing order),
   re-reads targets and recomputes `ageState` on the locked step, writes escalation OVERDUE with `escalationTargetSnapshot`,
   audited. Never automatic.
3. `exportCaseExtract(requisitionId)`: one REPEATABLE READ transaction; `asOf` = its start; reads the case tree, entries up to
   `asOf`, versions and access events; builds the file; writes `AuditExport` (scope CASE) and its audit row; returns the text, as
   `/configuration/audit` does. `recheckCaseExtract(exportId)` regenerates up to the stored `asOf` and compares digest and row count:
   "Matches the extract of 02 Oct 2026" or "Differs: 412 entries now, 411 then". The screen says an entry committed after the extract
   with an earlier time also shows as a difference. The re-check is audited.
4. `exportCommitteeReport(committeeId, from, to)` and `exportComplianceReport(from, to, unit?)`: server-built CSV, digest recorded
   (scopes COMMITTEE_REPORT, COMPLIANCE_REPORT), audited: the Board's and the regulator's files.
