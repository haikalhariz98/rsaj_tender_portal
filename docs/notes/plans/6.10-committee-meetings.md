# Plan: 6.10 Committee management, sittings, votes and minutes

Status **ruled, building**. The product owner ruled on section 5 on 2026-09-30
(`docs/notes/decisions-log.md`); where a ruling differs from what this plan first recommended,
sections 2, 3, 5 and 7 now carry the ruling. Spec:
§6.10 (FR-CTE-001 to 014), §7.3 Pending Committee / Committee Not Approved, §12 B4, B5, OQ-01
and §11.7.

**Read first:**
- **Mandatory acts require full attendance (see 5.9).**
- **NOT_CARRIED needs a configured not-approved outcome (5.5, ruled A).** With none configured the
  matter cannot be recorded and the sitting cannot be minuted; the blocker names what to configure
  and links there.
- **An unset voting rule blocks recording (5.6, ruled C).** No majority is assumed.

## 1. Requirements

Kept unchanged, with their tests untouched: `assessQuorum`, `tallyVotes`, `assessDecision` and
`seatsInForce`.

Defects in the existing code that this plan fixes:
- **D1** Ex-officio members cannot vote. The config page and `POST
  /api/committees/decisions/[id]/votes` only recognise seats with a `personId`, so the demo CEO
  and FD are shut out while the Secretary is offered as a voter.
- **D2** The votes route writes no audit row, never checks attendance, and stamps `votedAt =
  now()`.
- **D3** Nothing can record an outcome. `outcome` stays `PENDING`.
- **D4** The demo Tender Board has quorum 3 and only 2 voting seats, so it can never be
  quorate.
- **D5** `MeetingAttendance.standingInFor` is free text.

Rules that go with these signatures:
- **Mandatory acts (MR, MJR, MA).** The rule `assessDecision` already has is kept: every voting
  seat, less the recused, must vote. Since `canVote` refuses absentees, a mandatory act needs the
  **full voting body present** (5.9, ruled A). **A seat covered by a valid alternate counts as
  present** (ruling), because the check is on seats, not on named holders.
- **Voting rule (5.6, ruled C).** Unset gives `VOTING_RULE_UNCONFIGURED` and the matter cannot be
  recorded. SIMPLE_MAJORITY carries on more SETUJU than TIDAK SETUJU; UNANIMOUS carries only when
  every vote cast is SETUJU. A tie never carries. The rule and its provenance are snapshotted.
- **NOT_CARRIED (5.5, ruled A)** needs one of the configured, unretired outcomes offered to that
  committee. With none configured: `NO_OUTCOMES_CONFIGURED`, and the Callout says to configure one
  under Configuration → Committees → Not-approved outcomes and links there. Minuting waits.
- **ABSTAIN (5.7, ruled A)** is refused on input with 422. The enum value stays.
- **DEFERRED** needs a note but no quorum and no votes, because deferring is not a decision.
- **MINUTED** needs non-empty minutes and no PENDING matter.
- **CANCELLED** needs a reason and no CARRIED or NOT_CARRIED matter.

## 4. Screens and writes

**Writes.** Server actions in `src/app/committees/actions.ts`, using `FormReader` and
`auditedCreate`/`auditedUpdate`:
- schedule or edit a meeting;
- change status;
- record attendance;
- table a matter;
- declare a recusal;
- record an outcome;
- save minutes.

Each action runs the pure check first and saves nothing when the check refuses. A cancellation
asks for a reason. The meeting reference is drawn from `NumberingSeries` key `meeting` where one
is configured and typed otherwise. Issuance is not atomic (handoff.md).

**Votes route contract** (`POST /api/committees/decisions/[id]/votes`, hardened):
- Body: `{ personId: string, choice: "SETUJU" | "TIDAK_SETUJU", comment?: string, votedAt:
  "YYYY-MM-DD" }`.
- Responses:
  - 400: malformed input;
  - 404: decision not found;
  - 403: not a present voter, or recused;
  - 409: the meeting is MINUTED or CANCELLED, or the matter is already decided;
  - 422: ABSTAIN, or `votedAt` after today or before the sitting.
- Vote and audit row in the same transaction, with the before and after state.

**Navigation (5.1 A).** STAFF_NAV "Work" gains `{ href: "/committees", label: "Committee
sittings", icon: "users", built: true }`. **It replaces no NotBuilt page**, since none exists
for committees. It is a departure from the prototype, so add it to `ALLOWED_EXTRA` in
`scripts/compare-screen.mjs` with the reason.

- **`/committees`**: `PageHeader`, with facts for upcoming, awaiting minutes and matters pending.
  - `DataList` columns: committee label, reference, date, status `Badge`, matters, and quorum
    ("2 of 3" or "Not set").
  - Views: Upcoming / Awaiting minutes / All.
  - A "Schedule sitting" `Button` opens the drawer shell (`Field`, `Input`, `Select`).
- **`/committees/[id]`**: `PageHeader` with a status badge, facts (committee, date, quorum

  - **Attendance**: `Avatar` rows, a present toggle, and a "Standing in for" `Select`. Faults

## 5. Decisions (ruled 2026-09-30)

Each item carries the product owner's ruling first; the options are kept for the record.

1. **Where sittings live. Ruled A**: "Committee sittings" under Work at `/committees`, added to
   `ALLOWED_EXTRA` as a departure from the prototype.
   - A: top-level "Committee sittings" under Work. Operations stay out of setup, and it works
     before Phase 2.
   - B: a tab on the Configuration committee page. This mixes running with setup.
   - C: only on each matter's record, as in the prototype's approval tab. Not possible until
     Phase 2. It becomes an extra view later.
2. **"Vacate" on ex-officio seats. Ruled A with C's wording**: "Remove office from committee",
   reason required, ended by effective date, history kept.
   - A: offer it, ending the seat.
   - B: keep it hidden and use Edit.
   - C: offer it as "Remove office from committee", with a reason.
3. **Referencing a matter before requisitions and tenders exist. Ruled A** (ADR 0002), with
   `matterKind` a **closed set rendered by label**.
   - A: `matterRef` plus `matterKind`, and typed nullable FKs added per module. This keeps
     integrity.
   - B: a polymorphic `(type, id)`, which has no FK integrity.
   - C: no matters until Phase 2, which leaves the module unusable.
4. **Identifying the Secretariat without sign-in. Ruled A**: `secretariatRoleCode`, validated
   against offices on save; null refuses tabling; the tabler is shown as "attested".
   - A: `secretariatRoleCode`, an office resolved through RoleAssignment on the tabling date.
   - B: an OrgUnit. Rejected: Person has no OrgUnit link.
   - C: one organisation-wide office.
   - In every option the tabler is recorded as an attestation until sign-in exists. Null means
     tabling is refused.
5. **Not-approved outcomes (B4). Ruled A**, rejecting B′: NOT_CARRIED requires a configured
   outcome, because FR-CTE-009's acceptance requires a selection. Zero rows shipped; the blocker says
   what to configure and where; minuting waits.
   - A: a `NotApprovedOutcome` table with zero rows shipped, where NOT_CARRIED is blocked until
     one is configured. **The consequence:** a customer with no outcomes configured cannot
     record a matter that was voted down. The meeting then cannot be minuted, and the only
     escape, DEFERRED, misstates what happened. That is B4's "state with no exit".
   - B′: the same table, but NOT_CARRIED may be recorded with the outcome "not set". It carries
     a loud warning, blocks nothing downstream from advancing, and the outcome can be chosen
     later (audited).
   - C: ship the three SK003 options as defaults. This breaks "no customer policy ships".
6. **Combining votes (the spec is silent). Ruled C**: unset blocks recording
   (`VOTING_RULE_UNCONFIGURED`); no assumed majority. The decision snapshots the rule and its
   provenance. An assumed majority could later make a recorded CARRIED wrong.
   - A: a nullable `votingRule`. When unset, compute a simple majority and stamp the decision
     ASSUMED.
   - B: keep it hard-coded, as today.
   - C: when unset, block the decision the way an unset quorum does.
7. **ABSTAIN. Ruled A**: refused on input (422); the enum value stays for existing tests.
   - A: refuse it. FR-CTE-005 is binary, and recusal covers legitimate non-participation.
   - B: a per-committee switch.
8. **Secretary voting. Ruled A**: never votes or counts toward quorum. A product assumption,
   shown on screen, reversible later with a per-seat flag; a question for RSAJ with the Terms of
   Reference (B5).
   - A: never votes, a rule by position. This matches the prototype's "Out of {n} voting
     members".
   - B: a per-seat `votes` flag.
9. **Mandatory acts (MR, MJR, MA). Ruled A**, with the addition that a seat covered by a valid
   alternate counts (test added).
   - A: keep the existing rule. Every voting seat, less recusals, votes, which requires full
     attendance.
   - B: relax it to every *present* member, as for ordinary acts.
