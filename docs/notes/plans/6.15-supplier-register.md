### 0.1 Supplier identity: the central decision (OD-1)

The vendor workspace is the vendor's own acts. It needs to know **which company is asking**. Without sign-in nothing can answer that,
and FR-VND-013's "a vendor can never see another vendor's identity, price, score or documents" cannot hold on a page anyone can
open. Facts that bind the choice:
- Sign-in is **ruled a go-live condition for any customer** (decisions-log 2026-10-01 · Phase 1 · "Sign-in ruled a go-live condition
  … Building it stays deferred (AGENTS.md); this rules only go-live"). `phase1-acceptance.md` §6 item 10 still reads "Not ruled";
  that row is stale and should be corrected by the product owner.
- **Building sign-in is outside the product owner's remit.** `autonomous-build.md` "Never: Build sign-in, tenancy or maker-checker"
  and AGENTS.md "Do not stub auth" are founder rules. Option A below needs the founder to lift them.
- NFR-SEC-005 / INT-14: vendors authenticate against a **separate external identity store** from staff. So a supplier's people
  must never be `Person` rows (Person is staff and committee identity). Getting this wrong now forces a reshape later.
- Most of the sourced obligations are staff acts on paper today. These are eligibility (SK001 7.3.4(a)), document currency, the
  suspension check at sale (SK002 7.5.1(c)) and blacklisting (SK008 §7.9). Only the self-service *means* is net-new and D6-dependent.

| Option | What it is | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** Sign-in now | A platform module `platform-identity` (ADR) before 6.15: separate staff and vendor stores behind an `IdentityProvider` façade (INT-14; directory unknown, D5-like), `User`↔`Person` and `SupplierAccount`↔`Supplier`, sessions, MFA for approvers, re-auth at approve/sign/unseal (NFR-SEC-006). Then all of 6.15. | Unblocks the portal, maker-checker, SULIT restriction, NFR-AUD-004, and the authenticated halves of 6.7/6.8. It is a go-live condition anyway. | Breaks two founder rules. It is security-critical (needs an external review), the corporate directory is undecided, and it lands in the middle of a phase. The product owner cannot rule it. |
| **B** Register first | **6.15a now:** a staff-side supplier register (registration recorded from the paper application, documents, verification, review queue, expiry, standing, eligibility with the window check, special permission). It is wired into the 6.5 sale checks, 6.7 opening findings and 6.11 blacklisting. **6.15b/c after sign-in:** the external portal and the electronic box. | Delivers every sourced control and every carried-in hook without auth. The data model is designed so vendor accounts attach without a reshape. | §13.5's headline (self-service, less counter work) waits for sign-in. Typing paper applications is staff work. |
| **C** Portal with an "acting as" picker | The prototype's "Signed in for [company]" chooser. | Demonstrates well. | This is stubbing auth (AGENTS.md). It shows every vendor's data to anyone, the opposite of FR-VND-013. It is the §13.11 "ship the portal because it demos" failure. |

**Recommendation: B**, plus the founder's decision on A to be scheduled. Step 0 writes a **proposed** (unbuilt) ADR 0020 for
identity so that 6.15a's model is shaped for it. 6.15b and 6.15c are listed for the founder's end-of-build review as gated on sign-in
(founder), D6 and the SK002 §7.7 amendment (RSAJ), INT-10 (payment) and an external crypto review. **No `/supplier/*` page is
replaced in 6.15a**: they stay NotBuilt with their copy unchanged. Only `/suppliers` is replaced.

## 4. Actions (`src/app/suppliers/actions.ts`; hooks in their owner modules, each its own flagged commit with new tests only)

Lock order, one rule: **exercise side first, supplier last.** Supplier-only acts (§4.1, suspend, reinstate, blacklisting) lock
the supplier row, then the application/document/event. Any act touching both sides (`linkPartyToSupplier`, `grantSpecialPermission`,
the sale and opening hooks) locks requisition → exercise → window → party/sale, **then locks the supplier row** (FOR UPDATE) and
re-runs standing and eligibility on it, so a suspension cannot race a sale. No supplier-only act locks an exercise row. Policy and
reference values are re-read under the lock; every write is audited before/after; Persons are "recorded on behalf of, not signed".
**Award ↔ supplier:** where `Award.awardeePartyId` exists, the party's link is authoritative. `Award.vendorId` is written from it in
the same transaction, and a check refuses disagreement. `linkAwardToSupplier` is only for awards with no party (Phase 1, `sx10`–`14`).

1. a1: `recordApplication` (new supplier as APPLICANT, contacts, documents, declaration scan), `lodgeDocument`, `renewDocument`,
   `verifyDocument`, `submitForDecision` (opens the `supplier_registration` route, OD-2), `decideApplication`, `recordLapse`,
   `setErpVendorCode`. Configuration actions for bodies, values, types, reasons and policy (audited; destructive changes need a reason).
2. a2: `suspend`, `reinstate`, `requestBlacklisting` (route `supplier_blacklist`; from a 6.11 "blacklisting recommended" award it
   pre-fills `sourceAwardId`), `recordBlacklisting` on a decided route, `liftBlacklisting`, `linkPartyToSupplier`,
   `linkAwardToSupplier`, `grantSpecialPermission`.
3. Hooks: **6.5 `recordSale`** calls `saleStandingCheck` + `eligibility` and stores `eligibilitySnapshot`. **6.7 `openEnvelope`/
   finalise** freezes an eligibility finding for linked parties (OD-6). **6.11 cancellation** with CANCEL_AND_BLACKLIST offers
   `requestBlacklisting`.
