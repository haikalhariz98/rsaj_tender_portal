| Controlled reversal of an online sale (17b) | Product owner (for the founder), 2026-10-12, ruling 17b (wave 3a review M2). Without the simulator, the only payer of an online purchase is an officer typing the finance receipt, and a receipt typed against the wrong purchase gives documents and bid standing to a supplier who did not pay and uses up the number. Today the refusal to void stands and its words say so (the sale stands, it cannot be reversed in the portal yet, the opening still judges the buyer, a refund follows the organisation's own finance procedure). A go-live condition for online selling, a separate fix and not wave 3b. WHO: a two-person act. A signed-in officer proposes it with a reason and a different officer approves it, and the approver is not the officer who recorded the receipt, through an existing two-person pattern (the configuration change or the opening's dual authorisation; if neither fits, the ADR says why). WHEN: only while the window is open and the buyer holds no bid. EFFECT, in one audited transaction under the window lock: the sale is voided with its reason, the purchase moves PAID to VOIDED through a new transition the trigger explicitly allows, a raw-SQL partial unique index keeps one live purchase per supplier and window so the supplier can buy again, the release and bid standing end (downloads already made stay logged), and where the reversal corrects a receipt typed against the wrong purchase the number moves to the right purchase, which is awaiting payment, in the same transaction (the old value stays in the audit; the unique index on receipt numbers, OD-11, and the counter's one-person void are unchanged). MONEY: the product moves none. A refund reference is typed by an officer from finance and is never generated; with none the record reads "No refund recorded". Whether fees are refunded is the organisation's policy. A real provider's refund comes with its adapter (INT-10). | decisions log, 2026-10-12 ruling 17b; design 0003 ruling 17b |

Not part of the online reversal above, and not decided: the founder says whether a voided counter sale releases its number, and under what control. | decisions log, 2026-10-12 ruling 17b |
