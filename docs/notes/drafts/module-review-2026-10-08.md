## Common findings
1. Every tab shows for every method and stage.
2. No "what's next": screens say where a case is, not what it waits on or whom.
3. Work split across modules (e.g. bonds lodged on the award, shown read-only on the contract and the register).

## Sourcing
- 9–11 tabs, 16 sub-tabs; a quotation walks the same ~45–50 acts as a tender before publication.
- Direct appointments show Tender documents, Bids and Evaluation they never use.
- "Move to preparation" and "Start the tender documents" are two buttons for one job.
- Requisition lines are retyped into the bill of quantities; "What is delivered" and the proposed method are not carried over.
- Scope approval, late-bid admission and first-stage decisions are tabled by hand, not via "Waiting to be tabled".
- Specialist / sole source can never reach an award.
- Ideas: tabs by method and stage; "Next" line + one main button; one act to start preparing; carry-over from the requisition; committee items into the sittings flow; Evaluation 6 sub-tabs to 4.
- Questions: S1 quotation lighter than tender (per-method switch for the specification panel and Tender Table Document)? S2 specialist/sole source as a direct appointment with Technical Committee approval, or invite one firm? S3 direct appointment as a trimmed 4-tab exercise, or award from the requisition?

## Awards
- 9 tabs, two named "Routing sheet"; four header buttons in Draft (two "Edit"); "not ready to issue" appears only after approval.
- Blocker: the recommendation is SULIT; an officer without organisation-wide SULIT access and not named on the evaluation cannot open the award (ruling still OPEN).
- Ideas: "Still needed before issue" from Draft; 9 tabs to 5 (Overview, Approval, Letter, Securities and conditions, History); one main button per status; one-click "Award to the recommended tenderer"; post-issue work moves to the contract and the Awards menu item goes.
- Questions: A1 recommendation open to the awarding officer after the Board decides? A2 post-issue work on the contract, Awards menu removed? A3 suppliers accept and lodge bonds online?

## Contracts and Securities

- Questions: C1 go live with contracts stopping at "Active" (release and close are Must requirements)? C2 inbox notice on bond/insurance expiry, to whom? C3 securities lodged on the contract?

## Documents

- "Documents" means five things; each panel opens with an ~80-word access statement; each file shows twice; unfiled uploads and legal holds are under Configuration.
- Retention is computed but no disposal run exists.
- Ideas: rename (sidebar "Issued documents", contract tab "Certificates", configuration "Document rules"); compact panel everywhere; unfiled uploads and legal holds to a Records tab; remove the Review queue (reverses BD-614-31); later, one search across uploads with SULIT titles hidden.
